Can I request a tapered design and pricing on this please, if this one can be turned around asap as I belive works on site have already begun.

Bit of an odd one, but requirements are as follows.

Tapered insulation to fit in between structural upstands, Max 2.9m between them
Be 1:80 and tapered to each end from the centre
Minimum thickness of scheme, Including the 50mm flat board, of 120mm, to ensure it meets the results of our condensation risk analysis.

Below is some further details from Paul Rimmer of SIG Roofing.

I have attached a roof plan from which we need to secure a price as quick as possible for a CTF scheme in Powerdeck F. As the system is to be installed on Composite panel we need to quote as multi-layer with the
The first layer to be 50mm. The reason is the thickness of the outer sheet on the panel will be 0.5mm, less than the gauge of a minimum thickness steel deck so a pull out test has already been performed by SFS Fasteners and the fixing that passed was a peel rivet, so the 50mm is to be mechanically fixed and the tapered boards will be adhered to the 50mm, the on top tapered boards must be a single layer 1: 80 falls
To restrict the height build up. RTL have already been appointed contractor to this project and works have commenced with removal of roof top plant. There is an indication on the plan of the Structural Upstands
That for the purpose of costing we need to assume would be insulated with Flat 120mm board vertically and across the top. It would probably be better not to include these in the CTF scheme but as a separately quoted item.
